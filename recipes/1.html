Chicken liver recipe on YT

Chicken liver is one of the cheapest and most nutrient-dense protein sources available. It cooks in a pan in just a few minutes and is packed with both protein and iron to fuel muscle recovery.

Ingredients:

  • 500g chicken liver
  • 1 sliced onion
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice (or vinegar)
  • garlic
  • salt
  • pepper
  • cumin or coriander
