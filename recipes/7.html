Peanut Butter Oats recipe on YT

Perfect for breakfast or a pre-workout meal. Oats (flocon d'avoine) and peanut butter are easy to find. For a huge protein boost without expensive supplements, add a scoop of plain local Fromage Blanc (like Soummam or Petit Suisse).

Ingredients:

  • 1/2 cup rolled oats
  • milk or water
  • 1 tbsp peanut butter
  • 2 to 3 heaping tablespoons of plain Fromage Blanc (or yogurt)
  • honey or a banana for sweetness
