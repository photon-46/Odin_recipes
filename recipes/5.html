Beef & Eggs Skillet recipe on YT

Buying a small amount of ground beef (viande hachée) keeps the cost low, while adding eggs directly into the pan bulks up the meal and heavily increases the protein profile.

Ingredients:

  • 200g to 450g ground beef
  • 4 to 6 eggs
  • 1 chopped onion
  • garlic
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • salt
  • black pepper
