Butter Chicken Breast Bites recipe on YT

Cutting chicken breast into bite-sized cubes allows it to cook incredibly fast while absorbing the flavors instantly. It's a great way to make a smaller, more affordable cut of chicken feel like a massive meal when served with rice or pasta.

Ingredients:

  • 300g to 400g boneless chicken breast
  • 5 cloves of garlic
  • 1 to 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp oil
  • lemon juice
  • oregano or parsley
  • salt
  • pepper
