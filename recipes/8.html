Bean & Egg Shakshuka recipe on YT

White beans (Loubia) are incredibly filling. By cooking them down in a quick tomato sauce and cracking eggs on top, you get a dense, satisfying skillet that easily hits your calorie and protein targets for very little money.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup boiled or canned white beans
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 tin of peeled tomatoes (or 3 fresh grated tomatoes)
  • chopped onion
  • crushed garlic
  • paprika
  • olive oil
