Tuna Egg Omelette recipe on YT

When you need to eat immediately and don't want to wash a lot of dishes, combining tuna and eggs provides a massive, fast-absorbing protein hit with minimal cleanup.

Ingredients:

  • 2 to 3 eggs
  • 1 can of tuna (drained)
  • a pinch of salt and pepper
  • 1 tsp of oil or butter for the pan
  • onions or parsley if you have them
