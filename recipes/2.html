Chickpea & Tuna Salad recipe on YT

Zero cooking is required for this one. Canned chickpeas (Hommos) and canned tuna are inexpensive pantry staples that combine into a heavy, high-volume meal that you can prep in under 5 minutes.

Ingredients:

  • 1 can of tuna (drained)
  • 1 to 2 cups boiled or canned chickpeas
  • diced cucumber
  • diced tomato
  • red onion
  • lemon juice
  • olive oil
  • salt
  • pepper
